# Community Conduct

This repository welcomes good-faith technical and research participation.

Contributors and users are expected to:

- discuss technical disagreements respectfully;
- critique methods, code, evidence, and claims rather than people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive language;
- protect confidential and personally identifiable information;
- disclose relevant methodological limitations;
- avoid fabricated results, fake adoption claims, or manipulated citations; and
- respect software licenses and data-use restrictions.

Project maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these standards
or expose sensitive information.

For security-sensitive disclosures, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than posting
credentials or vulnerabilities in a public issue.
