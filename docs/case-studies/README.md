# Documented Case Studies

These case studies demonstrate how the research platform can be used to investigate
capital-readiness, fairness, geographic context, and allocation scenarios.

All applicant-level examples are **synthetic**. They are designed to make the analytical
workflow reproducible without implying that a real business was evaluated.

## Case studies

1. [Rural small-business capital access](01_rural_small_business.md)
2. [Low-income metropolitan entrepreneurship](02_low_income_metro.md)
3. [Growth-stage small business with constrained capital](03_growth_stage_business.md)

## Common workflow

Each case study follows the same research sequence:

1. define an illustrative business context;
2. review a synthetic capital-readiness score;
3. inspect local model sensitivity;
4. compare structural-context indicators;
5. examine geographic/public-data context where appropriate;
6. compare efficiency-only and equity-aware allocation scenarios; and
7. document limitations and questions for external validation.

## Research boundary

These are methodological examples, not credit recommendations, investment advice,
underwriting decisions, or evidence about any real business or community.
