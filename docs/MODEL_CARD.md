# Model Card

## Model name

Equitable Capital Optimization AI — baseline funding-success classifier.

## Version

0.2.0 research prototype.

## Model type

Random Forest classifier inside a scikit-learn preprocessing pipeline.

## Intended use

- research demonstration;
- software prototyping;
- fairness-metric experimentation;
- capital-allocation policy simulation; and
- teaching reproducible ML workflows.

## Out-of-scope use

The model is **not** intended for:

- lending or credit underwriting;
- investment selection;
- eligibility determination;
- employment decisions;
- housing decisions;
- insurance decisions; or
- any automated decision with legal or material effect on a real person or business.

## Training data

Synthetic startup and small-business records generated by this repository.

## Predictive inputs

Business operating and market characteristics including revenue, growth, runway,
employees, operating history, debt-service coverage, digital adoption, market demand,
management capacity, requested capital, state, and industry.

## Explicitly excluded predictive inputs

Protected personal characteristics and highly sensitive personal data are excluded.

## Evaluation

Holdout metrics:

- ROC-AUC;
- accuracy;
- precision;
- recall;
- F1; and
- Brier score.

Metric values vary deterministically with the configured synthetic seed.

## Explainability

Local sensitivity analysis and Random Forest global feature importance.

Neither should be interpreted as causal evidence.

## Limitations

- synthetic data does not establish external validity;
- the generated outcome is defined by the simulation itself;
- feature importance is model-specific;
- no temporal validation is performed;
- no calibration study is performed;
- no real-world subgroup or geographic validation is performed; and
- fairness diagnostics are descriptive rather than legal conclusions.

## Ethical considerations

A real system in this domain would require independent review of data provenance,
privacy, bias, model governance, human oversight, adverse-action requirements where
applicable, and the legal permissibility of any decision criteria.
